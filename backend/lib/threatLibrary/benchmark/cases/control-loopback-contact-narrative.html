Proxy implant tunnels through a local listener

The implant opens a local SOCKS listener and relays operator traffic to its upstream server. During staging the dropper wrote a second-stage sample with MD5 9c1e7a3b5d2f4e6a8c0b1d3f5a7e9c2b to the temp directory.
