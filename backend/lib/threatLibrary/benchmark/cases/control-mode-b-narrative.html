Credential stealer spreads through fake installer ads

Search advertisements lead victims to a fake download portal that serves a trojanized installer. After installation the stealer beacons to c2-update[.]info over HTTPS and exfiltrates browser credentials.

The installer abuses the Windows Credential Manager API, documented at https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/win32/api/wincred/, to enumerate stored secrets.

Recommendations

Restrict software installation to managed catalogs and monitor for credential-store enumeration.
